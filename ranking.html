RECORD RECOGNITION

Cloud records: connecting…

Record Maker (RM) and Record Breaker (RB) recognition can apply across MGR — staff, students, children, royal competitions, sports, skills, height, distance, speed, duration, donations and other measurable categories. The engine compares a new result with the established benchmark for that specific category.

RM — Record Maker

Closed padlock: the benchmark has been established and secured as an official record.

RB — Record Breaker

Opened padlock: the previous benchmark was broken and the new result becomes the secured record.
